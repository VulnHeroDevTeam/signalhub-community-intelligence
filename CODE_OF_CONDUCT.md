# Code of Conduct

SignalHub Community Intelligence welcomes respectful, evidence-based security research.

We expect participants to communicate constructively, cite claims, respect coordinated disclosure, avoid harassment and discrimination, and protect other people's confidential information.

Do not post credentials, private vulnerability details, threats, doxxing, malicious payloads, or instructions targeting systems without authorization. Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

To report conduct concerns privately, contact info@vulnhero.com with **SignalHub Community Conduct** in the subject.
