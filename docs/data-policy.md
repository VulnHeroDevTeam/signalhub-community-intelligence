# Public Data and Licensing Policy

SignalHub is a proprietary service. This repository may publish **selected, rights-cleared** research data; it is not a public export of all SignalHub records.

## Publication gates

Before publishing a record or dataset:
1. Verify right to redistribute the source material and contributor permission.
2. Check privacy, contractual confidentiality, embargoes and coordinated disclosure restrictions.
3. Review technical accuracy and provenance.
4. Minimize sensitive detail and exclude secrets, exploit payloads and identifying customer information.
5. Attach a dataset-specific license, attribution, source dates, and limitations.

**No blanket license currently applies to the repository.** Do not assume third-party feeds, NVD content, vendor advisories, or submitted PoCs can be republished without checking their terms.

Synthetic fixtures should be explicitly labeled as examples. Future datasets may use a suitable open-data license only after a rights review.
