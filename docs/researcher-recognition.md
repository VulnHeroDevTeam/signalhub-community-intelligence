# Researcher Recognition and Participation

The public SignalHub community model includes moderated research contributions, attribution, reputation and recognition.

## Principles

- Reward **accuracy, originality, reproducibility, and useful corrections**, not submission volume.
- Provide meaningful feedback on accepted, rejected, or revised contributions.
- Prevent duplicate sources or self-corroboration from artificially raising reputation.
- Allow researchers to challenge records and correct previous findings.
- Avoid incentivizing unsafe exploitation, premature disclosure, or spam.

## Proposed future ideas — not currently promised entitlements

Research opportunities matched to expertise; trusted researcher privileges; research-oriented access quotas; reviewer mentorship; thematic research campaigns; contribution impact reporting.

Any future benefits, thresholds, program terms, and eligibility rules require an explicit product policy before launch.
