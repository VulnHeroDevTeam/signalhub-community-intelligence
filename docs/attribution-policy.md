# Research Attribution Principles

When contributors permit public attribution, accepted original work should credit the researcher and link to the original reference where permissible.

Keep attribution distinct from endorsement. An accepted submission may validate the existence of a source without confirming the vulnerability is exploitable.

Suggested public metadata includes credited researcher handle (opt-in), contribution category, date, source link, review outcome and relevant corrections. Contributors must be able to raise attribution disputes and request review of mistaken credit.

Do not publish personal identity, private contact details or affiliation unless the contributor has consented and publication is appropriate.
